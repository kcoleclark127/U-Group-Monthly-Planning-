How We Do U-Groups

At Uproar Youth, we are disciples who make disciples. And a disciple is someone who is following Jesus, becoming like Jesus, and doing what Jesus did. That's the goal — and U-Groups are how we get there.

Every group should consistently hit three targets:

🎉 FUN — because joy opens doors
🫶 FAMILY — because trust breaks walls
✝️ FAITH — because Jesus is the center of it all

Each group — whether it's middle school girls or senior guys — has the same core ingredients, but the way it's baked will look a little different. It's like cake: same base ingredients (flour, eggs, sugar) — but add cocoa, and it's chocolate; add fruit, and it's strawberry shortcake. The core stays the same, but the flavor is unique to the students you're leading.

So every time you plan a group, ask yourself:

  • ✅ What's our fun hook?
  • ✅ What's our family moment?
  • ✅ How are we growing in faith?

This kind of intentionality will not only build strong disciples — it will build a youth ministry students want to bring their friends to.

Bucket List

Bucket List nights are the small, personal side of U-Groups — just 3–4 students getting real one-on-one time with a leader outside your regular meeting. Twice a month, pick something fun to do together and work in two intentional faith questions along the way. This is where the deeper relationships get built.

  • Twice a month (roughly every other week)
  • Don't repeat an activity from a prior month
  • 3–4 students per outing
  • 2 spiritual questions each time
